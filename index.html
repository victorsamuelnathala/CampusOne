The Complete Ecosystem

Nine Operational Modules. One Unified Login.

Purpose-built for day scholars, hostel residents, wardens, and facility managers—deployable modularly or all at once.

1. Issue Ticketing & Live Tracking

Photo-backed, geo-tagged campus issue reporting with real-time status progression from Submitted to Assigned to Resolved.

2. Indexed Lost & Found Network

Searchable, privacy-safe lost and found directory with automated item matching and verified campus security handovers.

3. Authoritative Official Notices

Verified, department-filtered announcements for exams, schedules, and deadlines—eliminating rumor mills and buried chats.

4. Hostel Maintenance Tracking

Dedicated residential workflows for electrical, plumbing, and Wi-Fi repairs with warden escalation rules and room-level history.

5. Structured Mess Feedback & Menus

Daily digital meal menus paired with structured hygiene and quality ratings to give catering committees actionable data.

6. Live Study Space Availability

Real-time occupancy visibility across libraries, seminar halls, and reading rooms—no hardware sensors required.

7. Digital Administrative Queues

Virtual token queuing for registrar, accounts, and scholarship offices with live wait-time alerts so students skip physical lines.

8. Lab Equipment Telemetry

Digital booking, calibration status, and fault logging for high-value laboratory instruments and departmental hardware.

9. Peer Academic Sharing

Moderated, course-indexed repository for verified lecture notes, study guides, and peer-to-peer resource exchange.
